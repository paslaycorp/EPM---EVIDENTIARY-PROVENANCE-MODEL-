# EPM Governance and Provenance

## Artifact classes

**Evidence** — an externally sourced observation or record accepted under the applicable evidence policy.

**Derivation** — a reproducible consequence computed from declared premises.

**Constraint** — a rule restricting an answer-space, with explicit provenance and assumptions.

**Inference** — a conclusion that goes beyond strict logical entailment and therefore requires its own epistemic characterization.

**Prediction** — an expectation about a future or unresolved outcome; never silently treated as evidence.

**Discriminator** — information or a test whose possible outcomes distinguish candidate answers.

**Invalidation** — a recorded event that removes or weakens support for an artifact.

## Provenance minimum

Each substantive artifact should preserve: stable identifier, author/source, creation time, input dependencies, transformation or reasoning method, status, supersession/invalidation information, and links to downstream dependents.

## Governance principle

No document in this research repository grants production authorization. Architecture and action policy require a separate decision with explicit scope.

## Change control

Research claims should be changed by additive, traceable commits. Do not silently rewrite history to erase failed hypotheses or counterexamples.
