# $1,000 EPM Break Prize

I released EPM v0.1.1 with a narrow promise: evidence should not gain applicability, authority, certainty, resolution, or temporal legitimacy that its evidence and governing context do not support.

Now I want someone independent to break that promise.

The first researcher who produces a complete, reproducible material invariant violation against the frozen v0.1.1 runtime wins **US$1,000**.

No opinion pieces. No vague objections. No moving target.

Use the exact release. Show the smallest executable reproduction. State the invariant. Show the observed result. Make it reproducible by someone else.

A clean attack does not prove EPM correct. A successful attack does not erase the architecture. Both outcomes become part of the evidence record.

Three outcomes:

**BREAK** — you violate a material invariant and the finding reproduces. First qualifying root cause takes the prize.

**EDGE** — you find a real ambiguity or weak boundary that stops short of a proven runtime violation. It gets recorded and investigated.

**HOLD** — you attack the boundary seriously and it survives. The failed attack becomes bounded validation evidence.
